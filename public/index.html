Photo requirements

  • Upright and framed like a portrait — head-and-shoulders, face clear, not sideways.
  • Face straight-on and clearly visible — well lit, no sunglasses or heavy filters.
  • Just you — anyone else must be cropped or blurred out.
  • Actually you — a real, recent photo of yourself. Not a celebrity or AI-generated.
  • Everyone 18+, nothing sexual — swimwear is fine; nudity is not.
  • You are anonymous while being rated — strangers see your photo and age, never your name. Your name and socials are revealed only to people you match with, and only if you choose.
