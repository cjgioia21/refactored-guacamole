## What you are agreeing to

If you accept this, **your photograph appears on public leaderboards inside
TrueHumanNature**, in a ranked list, alongside:

- your position in the ranking,
- the percentage of matchups you win,
- how many people have picked you and how many have passed you over.

**There are two boards, and you go on both.** One ranks the highest-rated
photographs. The other ranks the **lowest-rated** photographs.

You do not get to pick which one you appear on. Which board you land on is
decided entirely by how strangers rated you, and it can change at any time as
more votes come in.

## Say the obvious part out loud

**You may appear, with your face, at the top of a list titled as the worst-rated
people on this website.**

Other users will see it. They can screenshot it. Once someone has a screenshot,
it is out of our hands permanently — we cannot delete it, recall it, or stop it
being shared somewhere else.

People find this genuinely upsetting. Some people find it much more upsetting
than they expected to. If there is any part of you that thinks you might be one
of them, click no. You can always turn it on later.

## What we do to limit the damage

- The boards are visible **only to signed-in TrueHumanNature users**. They are
  not on the open web and not indexed by search engines.
- Photographs on them are served through the same expiring, session-locked links
  as everywhere else on the site.
- **Your name is never shown on the boards** — photograph and statistics only.
- You only become eligible once your photograph has been rated enough times
  (at least 50 matchups) that your position is not just noise.

Leaving costs you nothing and affects nothing else about your account.
