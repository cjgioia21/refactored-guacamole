## 1. What this site is, stated bluntly

TrueHumanNature shows your photograph to strangers and asks them to judge you.
They compare your face against other people's and pick who they find more
attractive. They guess things about you from your face. They answer questions
about you that are deliberately unkind, including whether they would abandon you
and whether they would cheat on a partner for you.

We then show you the results, including **rankings, percentages, and conclusions
that many people find upsetting** — for example your position in a ranked list of
all users, how many people passed you over, and how far your opinion of yourself
differs from theirs.

**This is the product. It is not a bug, and it is not something we will soften
because you dislike your result.** If being judged harshly by anonymous strangers
would distress you, do not create an account.

You also complete a questionnaire covering political views, sexual history and
attitudes, and optionally mental health. This produces a "Human Nature score"
that we use to decide who you are allowed to match with.
